Yu Chen (陈宇) 中文

I am currently a 1st-year Ph.D. student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), advised by Prof. Changsheng Xu in the National Laboratory of Pattern Recognition (NLPR). My research focuses on agent post-training, with the goal of enabling agents to perceive the world and make complex decisions more like humans. It spans three directions:

  • Long-horizon and self-evolving agents. Agents that stay coherent over long tasks and get better from their own experience instead of staying fixed after training.
  • Multimodal agents. Agents that perceive, reason, and act beyond text, such as Robot-Use Agents (RUA) that operate robots as tools and agents that reason over long videos.
  • Data pipelines for agent post-training. Scalable synthesis of tasks, environments, and verifiable trajectories, so that agent post-training is limited by compute rather than human annotation.

My perspective is shaped by both research training and hands-on industry experience. I believe impactful research should not only push the boundaries of knowledge, but also solve real problems with scalable, simple, and robust methods.

  • 面向 agent 后训练的数据管线。规模化合成任务、环境和可验证轨迹，让 agent 后训练受限于算力，而不是人工标注。

我的视角同时来自科研训练和一线工业经验。我认为有影响力的研究不仅要拓展知识边界，还要用可扩展、简洁、稳健的方法解决真实问题。
